NIMBUS ORBITAL PVT LTD - EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND WORK POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Owner: People Operations (HR)

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is credited at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried
forward to the next year. Days above 10 lapse on 31 December.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
needed only if sick leave is longer than 3 days in a row. Sick leave cannot
be carried forward.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers get 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
get 4 weeks. Parental leave must be applied for at least 30 days in advance,
except in emergencies.

4. WORK FROM HOME
Employees can work from home up to 3 days per week after completing their
probation period of 90 days. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM
IST, when everyone must be reachable on Slack.

5. INTERNET ALLOWANCE
Employees on a hybrid or remote plan get an internet allowance of INR 1,500
per month, paid with salary. Bills must be uploaded on the HR portal
(hr.nimbusorbital.example) by the 5th of each month.

6. LEAVE APPROVAL
All leave requests go to the reporting manager. If the manager does not reply
within 48 hours, the request is auto-approved. Leave longer than 10 working
days also needs approval from the department head.
